How Pratidaan works

1. GiveA donation and its screening results enter the demo record.
2. SeeEach blood component gets an anonymised journey and an impact Story.
3. UnderstandRules turn health findings into one clear Goal and next steps.
4. ReturnDrop checks in and shows when the donor can give again.

Care is available to donors who were turned away too. It is never payment for blood.

Features in the prototype

Impact and privacy

Component timelines, Stories, postcards, Badges and Circles. Recipient details are generalised until at least five patients share the description; dates are rounded.

Health and safety

Donation results, trends, Goals, Check-ins, eligibility countdown and an urgent Card. Fixed rules make clinical, privacy and cover decisions.

Drop and lab reports

Drop answers questions across the donor’s record in six languages. Azure OpenAI can extract markers from a lab report; the donor confirms before anything is saved.

Choices and data

Guest access, Microsoft sign-in, booking examples, optional Calendar/email flow, language choices and a Your data view with deletion controls.

Architecture in plain language

BrowserReact donor app, guest or Microsoft sign-in, screens and Drop chat
App ServiceNode API, synthetic demo data, safety and privacy rules, AI output checks
Azure servicesAzure OpenAI for language tasks; Blob for signed-in data; optional Graph for Calendar/mail

The browser calls the API. The API decides eligibility, findings, Goals, Card timing, cover illustrations and recipient privacy with deterministic code. It asks Azure OpenAI only for open-ended chat, report reading and translation. If a model call fails, rule-based responses keep the core journey available.

Prototype limits

All donor and patient records are synthetic. The app has no real blood-centre, hospital or insurer connection. Bookings and insurance cover are illustrative. It is not a medical device or a substitute for a clinician. A real pilot needs data agreements, clinical review and legal review of the cover model.
